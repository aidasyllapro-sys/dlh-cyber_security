# MedDefense Health Systems: The Board Pitch

**For:** Board of Directors **From:** James Chen, Deputy CISO **Re:** Approval of the $120,000 security investment

**Where we stand today:** After four weeks of assessment, MedDefense has zero of the 18 recognized industry security controls fully in place. Our internal network has no internal walls, meaning one compromised computer can reach every system we have, including patient records and financial data. This is fixable, and we know exactly how.

**What is at stake:** Our patient database currently accepts connections from any device on our network, not just the one system that should reach it. If exploited, a single incident of this kind carries an expected annual cost of just over $4 million once we count patient notification, legal exposure, and lost patient trust, a cost calculated the same way an actuary prices any real risk, not a guess.

**What we recommend:** We are requesting $95,800 of the $120,000 approved budget this year, held to $95,800 deliberately rather than spent to the last dollar. The top three actions, closing that database exposure, requiring a second login step for remote and administrative access, and installing a monitoring system that would have caught a threat we already missed once this year, together remove an estimated $4.6 million in annual risk on their own. Every dollar traces to a specific, already-identified danger, not a general sense that "security is good."

**What we gain:** For every $1 spent, MedDefense avoids an estimated $63 in expected annual loss. This is not a sales number; it is the same math behind the $24,200 we are deliberately keeping in reserve rather than spending, because we would rather ask for a specific dollar next quarter than one we cannot defend today.

We ask the Board to approve $95,800, not because it eliminates risk, but because it is the highest-return decision available to this organization this year.
