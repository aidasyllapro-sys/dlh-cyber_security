# Executive Brief: Security Incident IR-2026-0420-01

**Prepared for:** Board of Directors
**Presented by:** Dr. Morales
**Date:** Monday, 2026-04-21
**Classification:** Confidential — Board briefing

## What happened

Between Thursday and Sunday of last week, an outside party gained unauthorized access
to a number of our computer systems, including one server that stores certain patient
records. Our security team identified this activity Sunday evening and immediately
began cutting off the access the outside party had gained.

## What was affected

Patient lab results and clinical notes for a defined group of patients were viewed
without authorization. Based on our investigation so far, 1,847 individual documents
were accessed. We have not found evidence that financial or insurance payment
information was involved. We are still finalizing the exact list of affected
patients, which is required before we can notify them individually.

## What has not been affected

Clinical leadership was consulted before any computer was taken offline, specifically
to protect ongoing patient care during the response. Our investigation has not found
evidence that any patient's treatment was delayed as a result of our response actions.

## Regulatory posture

Based on a completed federal privacy law review, this incident meets the threshold
that legally requires us to notify affected patients and regulators, and that
notification process is now being finalized by General Counsel.

## What has been done

- All computers involved have been disconnected from our network to stop any further
  unauthorized access, confirmed complete as of Sunday night.
- The account used to gain this access has been disabled and its password reset.
- We have confirmed the outside party's connection to our systems has been fully cut
  off.
- A full technical investigation is underway to confirm the complete list of affected
  patients and the exact information involved.

## What remains

- Finalizing the exact list of affected patients so individual notifications can begin
- Completing the legal and regulatory notification process with General Counsel
- A full review of how the access occurred, to reduce the chance of recurrence

## Next update

The board will receive a follow-up update by **Wednesday, 2026-04-23**, or sooner if
material new information arises.
