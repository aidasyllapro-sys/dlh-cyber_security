# 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy establishes the rules and responsibilities for the appropriate use of TechSecure Solutions Inc. information systems, technology resources, and company data.

The purpose of this policy is to protect company assets, maintain confidentiality, ensure availability of systems, and reduce security risks caused by unauthorized or inappropriate use of technology resources.

This policy addresses acceptable usage of:

- Internet services
- Corporate email
- Software applications
- Social media
- Personal devices
- Company data

---

# 2. Scope

## 2.1 Applicability

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Temporary workers
- Remote workers
- Third-party users with authorized access

This policy applies across all TechSecure Solutions Inc. locations:

- Office locations
- Remote working environments
- Cloud-based working environments

---

## 2.2 Systems/Assets Covered

This policy applies to:

- Cloud-based development environments
- Corporate email systems
- Virtual Private Network (VPN)
- Collaboration platforms
- Company laptops and workstations
- Corporate networks
- Business applications
- Company data repositories

---

## 2.3 Exclusions

This policy does not apply to publicly available resources that do not interact with company systems or data.

---

# 3. Policy Statements

---

# 3.1 Internet Usage

Employees may use company internet resources for legitimate business purposes.

Limited personal internet usage is permitted when it:

- Does not interfere with work responsibilities.
- Does not consume excessive company resources.
- Does not violate company policies.

## Requirements

Employees must:

- Use internet access responsibly.
- Avoid accessing illegal, malicious, or inappropriate content.
- Avoid downloading unauthorized files.
- Avoid websites that create security risks.
- Not bypass security controls.

Internet activity may be monitored to ensure security, compliance, and proper resource usage.

---

# 3.2 Email Usage

Corporate email accounts are provided for business communication.

## Requirements

Employees must:

- Use company email primarily for business purposes.
- Protect confidential information sent through email.
- Verify recipients before sending sensitive information.
- Report suspicious emails or phishing attempts.

Employees must not:

- Use corporate email for unauthorized personal businesses.
- Send offensive, discriminatory, or illegal content.
- Forward confidential company information to personal email accounts.

Email records may be retained according to company retention requirements and legal obligations.

---

# 3.3 Software Usage

Only approved software may be installed or used on company systems.

## Requirements

Employees must:

- Obtain IT approval before installing software.
- Use only licensed software.
- Download software only from approved sources.
- Report suspicious software immediately.

Employees must not:

- Install unauthorized applications.
- Disable security software.
- Use pirated or unauthorized software.

The IT Security Team maintains the approved software inventory.

---

# 3.4 Social Media Usage

Employees must protect company confidentiality when using social media platforms.

## Requirements

Employees must:

- Protect confidential business information.
- Avoid publishing details about internal projects.
- Clearly state personal opinions when discussing company-related topics.
- Follow company communication guidelines.

Employees must not:

- Share customer information.
- Publish source code, screenshots, or internal documents.
- Represent the company without authorization.

Only authorized representatives may communicate officially on behalf of TechSecure Solutions Inc.

---

# 3.5 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal devices may only connect to company resources after approval and registration.

## Requirements

Employees using personal devices must:

- Register devices with IT.
- Enable device encryption where supported.
- Use approved authentication methods.
- Install required security applications.
- Maintain updated operating systems.

Employees must not:

- Connect unauthorized devices to company networks.
- Store confidential company information on unsecured devices.
- Disable required security controls.

---

# 3.6 Data Handling

Company data must be handled according to its sensitivity and business requirements.

## Requirements

Employees must:

- Store company data only in approved locations.
- Share information only with authorized individuals.
- Protect confidential and sensitive information.
- Follow secure disposal procedures.

Employees must not:

- Upload company data to unauthorized cloud services.
- Share confidential information externally without approval.
- Delete required business records.

Sensitive information must be securely deleted according to company retention procedures.

# 5. Compliance

## 5.1 Monitoring

TechSecure Solutions Inc. may monitor:

- Network activity
- Email systems
- Security logs
- Software installations
- Device compliance

Monitoring activities are performed to maintain security and compliance.

---

## 5.2 Reporting

Employees must immediately report:

- Security incidents
- Unauthorized access
- Lost devices
- Suspected malware
- Accidental data disclosure

Reports must be submitted through the company's security reporting process.

---

## 5.3 Auditing

Periodic audits may be conducted to verify compliance with this policy.

Audits may include:

- Software reviews
- Access reviews
- Device compliance checks
- Data handling assessments

---

# 6. Enforcement

## 6.1 Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Mandatory security training
- Removal of system access privileges
- Suspension
- Termination of employment
- Legal action where applicable

---

## 6.2 Reporting Violations

Suspected violations should be reported to:

Information Security Team

through the company's approved security reporting channel.

---

# 7. Exceptions

## 7.1 Exception Process

Exceptions require:

1. Written request submitted to the Policy Owner.
2. Business justification.
3. Security risk assessment.
4. Identification of compensating controls.
5. Formal approval.

---

## 7.2 Exception Duration

Approved exceptions must have:

- A defined expiration date.
- Periodic review.
- Documented renewal approval.
